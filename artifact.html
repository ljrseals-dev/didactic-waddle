Reverb

How it works

The vocoder renders first, so the machine voice is a normal mono recording. That recording goes through an HRTF panner for the direct sound, and into a shoebox-room model for reflections and the tail.

Direct level falls with distance (1/r). The diffuse tail stays the same level everywhere in the room, as in a real room. Where they are equal is the critical distance, drawn as the dashed circle.

Checked and not checked

Measured in headless Chromium with an impulse and noise bursts: a farther voice has a lower direct-to-reverb ratio; an off-axis voice produces level and time differences between the ears; larger rooms give longer reverb times.

Not checked: how it sounds, and whether the crossover really sits at the drawn critical distance. The room is a plain box with one absorption value for every wall. Nobody has listened to this.
